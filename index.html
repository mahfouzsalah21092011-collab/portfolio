Selected Case Studies

Two project examples showing cost awareness, technical follow-up and coordination with stakeholders.

Case Study 01 · Course 10 Mall

Water-supply route optimization

Location: 10th of Ramadan City, Egypt · Approx. 22,000 m² mall

Challenge

The originally considered main water-connection route was estimated at about EGP 5 million and would have required booster pumps.

Mahfouz’s responsibilities

Coordinated with the adjacent Hyper One site, assessed an alternative route with project stakeholders, arranged access and supported the technical decision to avoid the original route and planned booster pumps.

Approach

Identify a closer alternative main water-connection route while maintaining the project’s supply needs.

Reported result

The alternative route was reported to cost approximately EGP 750,000.

Case Study 02 · Souq El Madina Mall

Drainage and pump-system rehabilitation

Location: Egypt · Commercial mall / tenant drainage network

Challenge

Incorrect drainage installation and levels over a concrete slab led to recurring leakage.

Mahfouz’s responsibilities

Coordinated with mall management and the owner; followed the drainage correction, pump rehabilitation and operational adjustments with the relevant parties.

Approach

Reroute drainage lines at correct gradients, provide independent shop connections through inspection chambers, rehabilitate lift pumps and the control panel, adjust operating levels, alternate pump operation and add grease traps for restaurant tenants.

Reported result

The recurring leakage issue stopped after the corrective works, according to the project account.
