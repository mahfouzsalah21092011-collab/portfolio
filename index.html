Case Study 04 · Cross 10 Mall

Water-Supply Route Optimization

~22,000 m² Mall · Infrastructure Optimization

Challenge

The originally considered main water-connection route was estimated at ~EGP 5 million and required booster-pump stations.

Approach & Action

Coordinated with adjacent site stakeholders to identify a closer boundary connection route. Confirmed sufficient mains pressure, eliminating booster pumps and reducing connection expenditure to ~EGP 750,000 (indicative estimate).

Reported Result

Achieved permanent elimination of booster-pump energy and lifecycle maintenance costs.

Case Study 05 · Souq El Madina Mall

Drainage & Pump-System Rehabilitation

Commercial Mall · Drainage Remediation

Challenge

Incorrect drainage gradients and installation levels over a suspended concrete slab caused chronic leakage.

Approach & Action

Rerouted drainage lines at correct gravity gradients, provided independent shop inspection chambers, rehabilitated lift pumps and control automation, set alternating pump sequencing, and added grease traps for restaurant tenants.

Reported Result

The recurring leakage issue stopped completely following the corrective works, protecting structural and retail assets.
