A career combining electrical maintenance roots, full mall facilities management, and consultant-side MEP technical oversight.

Since 2010, Mahfouz has progressed from hands-on electrical maintenance of road equipment and municipal lighting into high-traffic facilities supervision, full commercial mall operations, and consultant-side MEP technical governance.

Through his work with Egyptian International Architecture Group (Mega Plan), he manages facilities and MEP technical oversight, supporting Retail Design & Delivery (RDD) and tenant fit-outs across 8 commercial mall developments in Egypt. Case Study 05 · Cross 10 Mall

Infrastructure Optimization & Water-Supply Route Engineering

~22,000 m² Mall · Infrastructure Optimization

Challenge

The originally considered main water-connection route was estimated at ~EGP 5 million and required dedicated booster-pump stations with permanent energy and maintenance overhead.

Approach & Action

Coordinated with adjacent site stakeholders to identify a closer boundary connection route. Confirmed sufficient municipal mains pressure, eliminating booster pumps and reducing connection expenditure to ~EGP 750,000 (indicative estimate).

Reported Result

Achieved permanent elimination of booster-pump energy and lifecycle maintenance costs while securing uninterrupted water supply to the mall.

Case Study 06 · Souq El Madina Mall

Drainage & Pump-System Rehabilitation

Commercial Mall · Drainage Remediation

Challenge

Incorrect drainage gradients and installation levels over a suspended concrete slab caused chronic wastewater leakage, risking tenant operations and structural integrity.

Approach & Action

Rerouted drainage lines at correct gravity gradients, provided independent shop inspection chambers, rehabilitated lift pumps and control automation, set alternating pump sequencing, and added grease traps for restaurant tenants.

Reported Result

The recurring leakage issue stopped completely following the corrective works, protecting structural and retail assets across the commercial facility.
